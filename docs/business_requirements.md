# Business Requirements

## Objective

Build an end-to-end Analytics Engineering solution for an e-commerce business.

## Business Questions

- How much revenue is generated over time?
- Which product categories perform best?
- Which customers generate the most value?
- Which sellers drive the most revenue?
- How do order volumes trend over time?

## Planned KPIs

- Revenue
- Orders
- Average Order Value
- Active Customers
- Revenue by Product Category
- Revenue by Seller
