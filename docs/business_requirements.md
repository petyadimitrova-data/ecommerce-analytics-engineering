# Business Requirements

## Objective

Build an end-to-end Analytics Engineering solution for a multi-vendor e-commerce marketplace using Snowflake, dbt, SQL, and GitHub.

The solution should provide trusted, well-documented data models and business metrics that support commercial performance analysis, customer analytics, logistics monitoring, and customer satisfaction reporting.

---

## Business Domains

### Commercial Performance

Provide visibility into sales performance across products, categories, sellers, and time periods.

### Customer Analytics

Enable analysis of customer acquisition, retention, purchasing behavior, and customer value.

### Logistics & Fulfillment

Monitor order processing and delivery performance to identify operational bottlenecks and opportunities for improvement.

### Customer Satisfaction

Measure customer sentiment and satisfaction through review analysis and its relationship to fulfillment performance.

---

## Business Questions

### Commercial Performance

* How much revenue is generated over time?
* How do order volumes trend over time?
* Which product categories generate the most revenue?
* Which products generate the highest sales volume?
* Which sellers generate the most revenue?
* What is the Average Order Value (AOV)?

### Customer Analytics

* Who are the highest-value customers?
* What is the repeat customer rate?
* What is the customer lifetime value (CLV)?
* How does customer value vary by geography?
* How are customers distributed across regions?

### Logistics & Fulfillment

* How long does it take orders to be approved?
* How long does it take orders to be delivered?
* What percentage of orders are delivered on time?
* Which regions experience the longest delivery times?
* Which sellers demonstrate the best delivery performance?

### Customer Satisfaction

* What is the average review score?
* What percentage of reviews are positive?
* How do review scores vary by product category?
* How do review scores vary by seller?
* Is delivery performance correlated with customer satisfaction?

## Success Criteria

The solution should:

* Provide a documented dimensional model aligned with business processes.
* Deliver trusted and reusable business metrics.
* Support analytical reporting across commercial, customer, logistics, and satisfaction domains.
* Demonstrate Analytics Engineering best practices using Snowflake, dbt, SQL, Git version control, and documentation.
* Serve as a portfolio-quality project showcasing end-to-end Analytics Engineering capabilities.
