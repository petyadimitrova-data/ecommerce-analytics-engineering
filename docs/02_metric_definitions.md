# Metric Definitions

### Question Answered:
How is business success measured?

### Purpose:
Define KPIs and metric calculations.

---

# Commercial Metrics

## Total Revenue

Business Definition:
Total value generated from customer purchases.

Business Purpose:
Measure overall marketplace sales performance.

Dimensions:

* Date
* Product Category
* Product
* Seller
* Customer Geography

---

## Total Orders

Business Definition:
Total number of customer orders placed.

Business Purpose:
Track marketplace demand and order volume trends.

Dimensions:

* Date
* Geography
* Seller

---

## Average Order Value (AOV)

Business Definition:
Average revenue generated per order.

Business Purpose:
Understand purchasing behavior and basket value trends.

Dimensions:

* Date
* Product Category
* Geography

---

## Revenue by Product Category

Business Definition:
Revenue aggregated by product category.

Business Purpose:
Identify top-performing product categories.

Dimensions:

* Product Category
* Date

---

## Revenue by Seller

Business Definition:
Revenue attributed to individual marketplace sellers.

Business Purpose:
Identify top-performing sellers and seller concentration.

Dimensions:

* Seller
* Geography
* Date

---

# Customer Metrics

## Active Customers

Business Definition:
Number of unique customers placing orders during a selected period.

Business Purpose:
Measure customer engagement and marketplace activity.

Dimensions:

* Date
* Geography

---

## Repeat Customer Rate

Business Definition:
Percentage of customers who place more than one order.

Business Purpose:
Measure customer retention and loyalty.

Dimensions:

* Geography
* Customer Segment

---

## Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)

Business Definition:
Total revenue generated by a customer across all purchases.

Business Purpose:
Identify high-value customers and customer segments.

Dimensions:

* Customer
* Geography

---

# Logistics Metrics

## Average Approval Time

Business Definition:
Average time between order placement and order approval.

Business Purpose:
Measure order processing efficiency.

Dimensions:

* Date
* Seller

---

## Average Delivery Time

Business Definition:
Average time between order placement and successful delivery.

Business Purpose:
Measure fulfillment performance.

Dimensions:

* Seller
* Geography

---

## On-Time Delivery Rate

Business Definition:
Percentage of orders delivered on or before the estimated delivery date.

Business Purpose:
Measure delivery reliability.

Dimensions:

* Seller
* Geography

---

## Average Delivery Delay

Business Definition:
Average number of days delivered after the estimated delivery date.

Business Purpose:
Identify delivery performance issues.

Dimensions:

* Seller
* Geography

---

# Customer Satisfaction Metrics

## Average Review Score

Business Definition:
Average customer review score across completed orders.

Business Purpose:
Measure overall customer satisfaction.

Dimensions:

* Product Category
* Seller
* Geography

---

## Positive Review Rate

Business Definition:
Percentage of reviews considered positive based on review score thresholds.

Business Purpose:
Track customer sentiment and satisfaction trends.

Dimensions:

* Product Category
* Seller
* Geography
